Matara District

Capital: Matara | Southern Province

Area: 1,282 km²
6 Curated Destinations
GPS: 5.95°N, 80.56°E

Geographical Character & Overview

World-renowned whale watching off Mirissa, picturesque Coconut Tree Hill, beginner-friendly surf at Weligama, and the island's southernmost lighthouse.

Golden beaches, sheltered horseshoe bays, coastal rocky promontories, and Nilwala River basin.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Grilled jumbo prawns, spicy fish ambul thiyal, tropical smoothie bowls, and freshly caught tuna steaks.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

Blue whale & sperm whale watching boat trips,Surfing Weligama beach breaks,Snorkeling with sea turtles at Polhena,Climbing Dondra Lighthouse

Marine & Diving Attractions
Matara District

Mirissa Beach & Whale Watching Sanctuary

The world's premier departure point for observing colossal Blue Whales and sperm whales in their natural habitat, paired with a lively crescent bay.

Half Day to Full Day (boat trips depart 06:30 and return 10:30 - 12:30).

Coconut Tree Hill Mirissa

The world-famous promontory near Mirissa, where a grove of towering coconut palms overlooks the crashing Indian Ocean from a red-clay cliff.

45 Minutes to 1 Hour

Beaches & Coastlines
Matara District

Weligama Bay Surf Haven

South Asia's premier learn-to-surf beach, a vast crescent bay with gentle rolling sandy-bottom waves, surf schools, and the private islet of Taprobane.

Half Day to Multi-Day Surf Camp
