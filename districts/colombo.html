Colombo District

Capital: Colombo | Western Province

Area: 699 km²
8 Curated Destinations
GPS: 6.93°N, 79.86°E

Geographical Character & Overview

The dynamic coastal financial hub and cultural crossroads of Sri Lanka, where colonial architecture meets oceanfront promenades and lush urban wetlands.

Coastal lowlands, Kelani River estuary, urban lagoons, and protected marshy wetlands.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Isso Vadai on Galle Face Green, Kottu Roti at midnight diners, Lamprais (Dutch-Burgher wrapped rice), and fresh lagoon seafood.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

Sunset catamaran rides,Urban wetland birding tours,Heritage open-top bus explorations,Scuba diving historic wrecks off Colombo coast
