Hambantota District

Capital: Hambantota | Southern Province

Area: 2,609 km²
8 Curated Destinations
GPS: 6.12°N, 81.12°E

Geographical Character & Overview

The wild safari capital of the south, home to leopard haven Yala, flamingos of Bundala, Hummanaya blowhole, and the holy shrine of Kataragama.

Arid scrub plains, coastal dunes, saline lagoons (lewayas), rocky ridges, and dry-zone forests.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Buffalo curd with kithul treacle in clay pots, roasted fish with chili sambol, and local millet (kurakkan) flatbreads.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

Dawn and dusk 4x4 safaris in Yala,Night turtle nesting watch at Rekawa,Surfing the horseshoe reef at Hiriketiya,Birdwatching boat tours in Bundala lagoons

Hummanaya Blowhole (Kudawella)

The second largest natural sea blowhole in the world, shooting ocean geysers up to 30 meters into the sky through a narrow rock fissure with a roaring hiss.

Yala National Park (Ruhuna)

Sri Lanka's flagship wildlife sanctuary, boasting one of the highest densities of leopards on Earth, alongside Asian elephants, sloth bears, and crocodiles.
