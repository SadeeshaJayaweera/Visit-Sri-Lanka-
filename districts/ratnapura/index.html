Ratnapura District

Capital: Ratnapura | Sabaragamuwa Province

Area: 3,275 km²
7 Curated Destinations
GPS: 6.68°N, 80.40°E

Geographical Character & Overview

The world's 'City of Gems', the holy pilgrimage peak of Adam's Peak (Sri Pada), and the virgin canopy of Sinharaja Rainforest.

Rugged cloud forest mountains, virgin rainforest valleys, cascading waterfalls, and gem-gravel river floodplains.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Hot kithul jaggery with freshly grated coconut, village rice and curry with fresh garden vegetables, and spicy river crab.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

Night pilgrimage climb to the summit of Adam's Peak,Guided birdwatching trek through Sinharaja Rainforest,Safari with hundreds of elephants in Udawalawe,Visiting traditional pit gem mines

Mountains & Highlands
Ratnapura District

Adam's Peak (Sri Pada / Sacred Footprint Mountain)

The sacred 2,243-meter pyramid mountain venerated by four world religions for the holy footprint atop its summit, famous for its midnight pilgrimage and mystical sunrise shadow.

6 - 8 Hours (Ascent: 3.5 - 5 hours starting at 01:00 AM; Descent: 2.5 - 3.5 hours).

Forests & Nature Reserves
Ratnapura District

Sinharaja Forest Reserve (UNESCO Biosphere)

The last viable expanse of virgin tropical lowland rainforest in Sri Lanka, a UNESCO World Heritage biodiversity treasure where over 60% of trees and 95% of birds are endemic.

Half Day to Full Day (or Multi-Day eco-lodge stay)

Waterfalls
Ratnapura District

Bopath Ella Bo-Leaf Waterfall

A picturesque 30-meter waterfall that cascades through a narrow rocky cleft and widens into the distinctive shape of a sacred Bo leaf (Ficus religiosa).

1.5 - 2.5 Hours

Cultural Experiences
Ratnapura District

Ratnapura City of Gems & Traditional Mines

The world's premier gem trading capital, where traditional bamboo-shored pit mines unearth the world's finest royal blue sapphires, rubies, and alexandrites.

2 - 3 Hours

Hidden Gem
Ratnapura District

Maha Saman Devalaya

The grand 13th-century guardian shrine of God Saman, protector of Sri Pada and Sabaragamuwa, home to the magnificent annual Saman Devalaya Perahera.

Udawalawe National Park & Elephant Transit Home
