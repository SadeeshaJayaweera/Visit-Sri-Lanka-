Puttalam District

Capital: Puttalam | North Western Province (Wayamba)

Area: 3,072 km²
7 Curated Destinations
GPS: 8.04°N, 79.83°E

Geographical Character & Overview

Asia's premier kitesurfing mecca at Kalpitiya, superpods of spinner dolphins, Bar Reef coral gardens, and the vast wilderness of Wilpattu.

Sandy coastal spits, tranquil lagoon bays, extensive salt pans, mangrove wetlands, and dry monsoon forests.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Spicy buttered squid, fresh crab curry, salt-cured lagoon fish, and savory roti flatbreads.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

Kitesurfing in Kalpitiya Lagoon & flatwater spots,Dolphin and whale watching boat expeditions,Snorkeling at Bar Reef,Birdwatching in Anawilundawa wetlands

Puttalam District

Kalpitiya Peninsula & Dolphin Watching

Asia's premier kitesurfing destination and marine mammal sanctuary, where boat tours encounter massive pods of hundreds of acrobatic spinner dolphins.

Historical Sites
Hidden Gem
Puttalam District

Kalpitiya Dutch Fort

A 1676 Dutch fortress constructed of coral and limestone, built to dominate the lucrative cinnamon and areca-nut trade in the Puttalam Lagoon.

1 Hour

Marine & Diving Attractions
Hidden Gem
Puttalam District

Bar Reef Marine Sanctuary

The largest and most biodiverse coral reef system in Sri Lanka, spanning 307 square kilometers of pristine offshore coral gardens, manta rays, and reef sharks.

3 - 5 Hours (boat departure from Kalpitiya)

National Parks
Puttalam District

Wilpattu National Park (Land of Lakes & Leopards)

Sri Lanka's largest and oldest national park, famous for 106 natural sand-rimmed rain-fed lakes ('villus'), dense monsoon forests, leopards, and sloth bears.

Half Day to Full Day Safari (Full day: 06:00 - 18:00 recommended due to vast park scale).

Forests & Nature Reserves
Hidden Gem
Puttalam District

Anawilundawa Wetland Ramsar Sanctuary

A magnificent 1,397-hectare Ramsar wetland of seven ancient cascading irrigation tanks dating back to the 12th century, harboring over 150 bird species.

2 - 3 Hours

Alankuda Beach

A windswept, peaceful 2-kilometer golden beach on the Kalpitiya peninsula, famous for rustic eco-lodges, wind power turbines, and direct boat safaris for dolphin watching.
