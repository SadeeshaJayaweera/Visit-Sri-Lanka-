Kurunegala District

Capital: Kurunegala | North Western Province (Wayamba)

Area: 4,816 km²
5 Curated Destinations
GPS: 7.49°N, 80.36°E

Geographical Character & Overview

Land of seven monumental animal-shaped rocks, the soaring stone staircase of Yapahuwa rock citadel, and the ancient Silver Temple.

Towering granite inselbergs rising above fertile coconut estates and ancient irrigation lakes.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Traditional Wayamba village curry, kurakkan thalapa (finger millet dough) with country chicken curry, and fresh young king coconut.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

Climbing the sheer stone staircase of Yapahuwa,Hiking up Athugala Rock to the giant Buddha statue,Exploring ancient cave shrines of Ridi Viharaya

Kurunegala District

Yapahuwa Ancient Rock Citadel & Staircase

The 13th-century rock citadel rising 100 meters above the plains, famous for its magnificent ornamental stone stairway carved with Chinese-influenced lions and dancers.

Ridi Viharaya (Silver Temple) Cave Monastery

An ancient 2nd-century BCE rock cave monastery founded where a miraculous silver vein was discovered to finance the construction of the Great Ruwanwelisaya stupa.
