Kalutara District

Capital: Kalutara | Western Province

Area: 1,598 km²
4 Curated Destinations
GPS: 6.59°N, 79.96°E

Geographical Character & Overview

Known for the majestic hollow Buddhist stupa of Kalutara Bodhiya, Kalu Ganga river adventures, colonial mansions, and fragrant mangosteens.

River deltas, coastal sandbars, tranquil freshwater lakes, and fertile rubber and mangosteen orchards.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Fresh sweet mangosteens, dodol (caramelized coconut jelly), fresh river fish, and coconut arrack.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

River boat cruises along Kalu Ganga,Water skiing on Bolgoda Lake,Cycling through rubber estates

Kalutara District

Kalutara Bodhiya & Hollow Stupa

One of Sri Lanka's most venerated Buddhist pilgrimage shrines, featuring a hollow stupa with painted interior murals, located on the banks of the Kalu Ganga.
