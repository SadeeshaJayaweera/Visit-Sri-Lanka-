Kandy District

Capital: Kandy | Central Province

Area: 1,940 km²
6 Curated Destinations
GPS: 7.29°N, 80.63°E

Geographical Character & Overview

The sacred spiritual capital of Sri Lanka, sheltering the revered Temple of the Tooth Relic, royal botanical wonderlands, and cloud forest peaks.

Mountain valleys, terraced paddy fields, highland tea slopes, and cloud-draped mountain passes.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Authentic Kandyan sweetmeats (kavum, kokis), polos (young jackfruit curry), and highland Ceylon breakfast tea.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

Trekking Knuckles High Range,Climbing Ambuluwawa spiral tower,Scenic railway journeys to Ella,Peradeniya canopy walks
