Badulla District

Capital: Badulla | Uva Province

Area: 2,861 km²
12 Curated Destinations
GPS: 6.99°N, 81.06°E

Geographical Character & Overview

The adventure trekking capital of Ella, the iconic Demodara Nine Arch Bridge, thunderous waterfalls, and tea-blanketed peaks.

High mountain ridges, deep gaps, cascading waterfalls, tea estates, and pine and cloud forests.

Local Food & Culinary Specialties

Ella cafe smoothie bowls, spicy devilled cashews, handmade roti with seeni sambol, and single-estate Haputale tea.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

Watching the train cross Nine Arch Bridge,Swimming in Diyaluma upper natural rock pools,Hiking Ella Rock and Little Adam's Peak,Zip-lining across the Ella valley

Ella Rock Summit Trek & Ella Gap

The ultimate hiking challenge in Ella, a rewarding 4-hour trek along active railway tracks, through rubber and eucalyptus forests, to a dizzying 1,000m sheer cliff precipice.

A spectacular 25-meter tiered roadside waterfall cascading over oval-shaped concave rock ledges, steeped in the ancient Ramayana legend of King Ravana and Sita.
