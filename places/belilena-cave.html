Belilena Prehistoric Cave Site

A colossal prehistoric rock shelter near Kitulgala that yielded 30,000-year-old skeletal remains of Balangoda Man and early geometric microlith tools.

Caves & Caverns
Kegalle District
Best Season: December to April (drier trail conditions); morning visits provide comfortable temperatures.
Duration: 2 - 3 Hours
Hidden Gem

About Belilena Prehistoric Cave Site

Perched on a forested mountain ridge 8 kilometers from Kitulgala town, Belilena (meaning 'Beli Shell Cave') is one of the most culturally significant prehistoric archaeological sites in South Asia. Measuring 60 meters wide and fronted by a natural subterranean waterfall curtain, this immense limestone cave shelter was continuously inhabited by prehistoric hunter-gatherers (Homo sapiens balangodensis) from 30,000 to 9,000 years before the present. Groundbreaking excavations conducted by the Department of Archaeology uncovered 10 human skeletons dating back 16,000 to 30,000 years, along with fire hearths, charred bones of jungle fowl and monkeys, bone awls, and the famous geometric microliths that proved early humans in Sri Lanka possessed advanced toolmaking abilities thousands of years earlier than previously believed in Europe. Reaching the cave requires a scenic trek through rubber estates and mountain rainforest.

Historical & Cultural Background

Excavated between 1978 and 1983 by Dr. Siran Deraniyagala; recognized as a crucial paleoanthropological site of early modern human migration.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

Immense limestone cave shelter situated at 610m elevation on a steep rainforest mountain ridge.

What Makes It Special

30,000-year-old human habitation strata, natural waterfall curtain across the cave mouth, and bone tool discoveries.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Stand inside an immense rock shelter where prehistoric humans lived 30,000 years ago
  • Marvel at the natural mountain waterfall that cascades directly in front of the cave entrance
  • Learn about the groundbreaking archaeological excavations of Balangoda Man
  • Enjoy a quiet, rewarding nature hike through rubber and tea plantations near Kitulgala

Things to Do & Available Experiences

Prehistoric cave exploration
Archaeological study
Rainforest nature hiking

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Sturdy walking shoes, flashlight or headlamp, drinking water, insect repellent.
Recommended Clothing: Comfortable hiking clothes.
Photography Advice: Photograph looking out from inside the cavern through the curtain of falling waterfall spray into the green forest canopy.
Local Customs: Quiet, educational historical site.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Strictly do not deface the cave walls or disturb the archaeological earth layers.
