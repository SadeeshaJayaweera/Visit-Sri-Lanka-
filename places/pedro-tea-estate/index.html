Pedro Tea Estate & Factory

One of Sri Lanka's oldest operating tea factories established in 1885, where visitors can witness traditional orthodox Ceylon tea processing and sample Lovers Leap tea.

Tea Plantations & Estates
Nuwara Eliya District
Best Season: Year-round; morning visits (08:30 - 11:30) when the factory is actively processing the morning harvest.
Duration: 1.5 - 2 Hours
Popular Highlight

About Pedro Tea Estate & Factory

Nestled 3.5 kilometers east of Nuwara Eliya town at the foot of Mount Pidurutalagala, Pedro Tea Estate is an operational living museum of orthodox Ceylon tea manufacture. Established in 1885 during the British colonial era, the factory continues to utilize original 19th-century machinery to produce the delicate, light, aromatic high-grown teas characteristic of the Nuwara Eliya terroir. Visitors join an engaging 30-minute guided factory tour (wearing white visitor aprons) explaining the orthodox five-step tea-making process: withering, rolling, fermentation, firing, and grading. Following the tour, guests enjoy a fresh pot of steaming Lovers Leap single-estate black tea on the factory's open-air verandah, overlooking rolling emerald tea bushes and the distant cascades of Lovers Leap waterfall.

Historical & Cultural Background

Established in 1885; still preserves historic British-era manufacturing machinery and orthodox production techniques.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

High-altitude tea estate (1,900m elevation) situated on the mountain slopes of Mount Pidurutalagala.

What Makes It Special

1885 operating tea factory machinery, guided orthodox tea processing tours, and Lovers Leap tea tasting terrace.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Learn the fascinating science and art of authentic orthodox Ceylon tea production from leaf to cup
  • Witness original 19th-century British tea machinery still operating in daily production
  • Savor a freshly brewed cup of premium Lovers Leap high-grown tea on the scenic garden terrace
  • Walk through neat tea fields and meet tea pluckers harvesting the 'two leaves and a bud'

Things to Do & Available Experiences

Guided tea factory tours
Tea tasting masterclasses
Walking through tea estates
Visiting nearby Lovers Leap waterfall

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Warm clothing, comfortable walking shoes, camera.
Recommended Clothing: Casual comfortable attire; visitor coats are provided for factory floor entry.
Photography Advice: Photograph the sea of green tea bushes rolling up the slopes of Pidurutalagala from the cafe verandah.
Local Customs: A polite tip for the tea factory guide is appreciated.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Do not damage tea bushes or walk into restricted harvest plots without permission.
