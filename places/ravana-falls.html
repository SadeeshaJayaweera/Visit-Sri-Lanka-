Ravana Falls & Ravana Cave

A spectacular 25-meter tiered roadside waterfall cascading over oval-shaped concave rock ledges, steeped in the ancient Ramayana legend of King Ravana and Sita.

Waterfalls
Badulla District
Best Season: June to November during and following monsoon rains for maximum water volume; morning visits (08:30 - 11:00) offer pleasant light.
Duration: 45 Minutes to 1.5 Hours
Popular Highlight

About Ravana Falls & Ravana Cave

Tumbling dramatically down a concave rock amphitheater along the scenic Ella–Wellawaya mountain road (A23), Ravana Falls (Ravana Ella) is one of Sri Lanka's most popular and accessible waterfalls. Plunging approximately 25 meters (82 feet) in multiple cascading tiers, the waterfall is fed by the Kirindi Oya flowing down the Ella valley. The falls are steeped in the ancient Ramayana epic: according to legend, the 10-headed King Ravana of Lanka kidnapped Princess Sita from India and concealed her in the subterranean caverns of the nearby Ravana Cave behind the curtain of rushing water. Today, travelers stop at the roadside viewing bridge to admire the thunderous white cascades, watch troops of playful toque macaque monkeys in surrounding trees, and purchase fresh tropical fruit from local stalls.

Historical & Cultural Background

Deeply connected to the Ramayana epic; Ravana Cave located 2 km upstream was an ancient prehistoric human habitation cave dating to 25,000 BCE.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

Tiered cascade falling over rounded concave limestone rock tiers fed by the Kirindi Oya river.

What Makes It Special

Spectacular tiered cascades directly accessible from the highway, Ramayana epic legend, and nearby 25,000-year-old Ravana Cave.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Admire a roaring 25-meter multi-tiered waterfall directly from a roadside viewing bridge
  • Learn about the legendary connection to King Ravana and Princess Sita in the Ramayana epic
  • Hike to the nearby Ravana Cave and Nil Diya Pokuna underground lake
  • Taste fresh tropical pineapples, sweet bananas, and roasted corn at roadside stalls

Things to Do & Available Experiences

Waterfall viewing and photography
Visiting nearby Ravana Cave (650 steps)
Sampling roadside tropical fruit
Monkey watching

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Camera with fast shutter speed, lens wipe (spray can reach the bridge), small cash notes.
Recommended Clothing: Casual comfortable travel clothes.
Photography Advice: Photograph the multi-tiered cascades from the viewing platform on the opposite side of the road to frame the entire height.
Local Customs: Keep snacks and food items secured from mischievous monkeys around the bridge.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Strictly do not discard plastic bottles or food wrappers into the river.

Visitor Information

District Badulla District
Province Uva Province
Opening Hours Open 24 hours.
Entrance Fee Free public viewing of the waterfall from the road bridge; Ravana Cave entrance fee is approx. LKR 500.
Best Season June to November during and following monsoon rains for maximum water volume; morning visits (08:30 - 11:00) offer pleasant light.
Visiting Duration 45 Minutes to 1.5 Hours
Accessibility Roadside viewing bridge is paved and fully accessible for vehicles and wheelchairs; climbing to upper cascade pools is rocky and hazardous.
Safety Advice Do not climb onto wet, algae-covered rocks near the waterfall base; flash floods can occur upstream during sudden mountain rains; swimming in deep upper pools is prohibited.
GPS Coordinates 6.8400° N, 81.0500° E
