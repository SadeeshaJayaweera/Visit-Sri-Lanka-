Kalutara Bodhiya & Hollow Stupa

One of Sri Lanka's most venerated Buddhist pilgrimage shrines, featuring a hollow stupa with painted interior murals, located on the banks of the Kalu Ganga.

Temples & Religious Sites
Kalutara District
Best Season: Early morning or twilight (17:30 - 19:30) when the white stupa is dramatically illuminated.
Duration: 1 - 1.5 Hours
Popular Highlight

About Kalutara Bodhiya & Hollow Stupa

Dominating the southern bank of the Kalu Ganga river where the Galle Road bridge enters Kalutara town, the Kalutara Bodhiya is an iconic spiritual landmark. The sacred site centers around a venerable Bodhi tree, believed to be one of the 32 saplings of the sacred Anuradhapura Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi planted in the 2nd century BCE. In the 1970s, the Kalutara Bodhi Trust completed the Kalutara Chaitya—a world-unique hollow Buddhist stupa engineered so that visitors can step directly inside its cavernous interior to view a smaller central stupa and 74 ceiling murals illustrating the Jataka tales. Drivers and commuters traditionally pause here to place coins in roadside charity boxes for a safe journey.

Historical & Cultural Background

A sacred Bo sapling was planted here during the reign of King Devanampiya Tissa (3rd century BCE); the hollow stupa was completed in 1974.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

Perched on a hillock overlooking the wide estuary of the Kalu Ganga and the Indian Ocean.

What Makes It Special

World's only major completely hollow stupa, vibrant interior Jataka murals, and panoramic river views.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Walk inside the world's most famous hollow Buddhist stupa and admire 74 painted Jataka murals
  • Pay respects at one of the sacred 32 saplings of the ancient Anuradhapura Sri Maha Bodhi
  • Enjoy panoramic views of the Kalu Ganga meeting the Indian Ocean from the upper terrace
  • Witness the daily devotion of thousands of island travelers pausing for blessing coins

Things to Do & Available Experiences

Temple worship
Viewing Buddhist mural paintings
Riverfront terrace photography
Meditation

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Modest white or light-colored attire, slip-off footwear.
Recommended Clothing: Shoulders and knees must be respectfully covered.
Photography Advice: Photograph the glowing white stupa from the Kalu Ganga bridge at sunset.
Local Customs: Walk clockwise (padakshina) around the central stupa inside the hollow dome.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Respect the tranquility of devotees in prayer.
