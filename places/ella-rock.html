Ella Rock Summit Trek & Ella Gap

The ultimate hiking challenge in Ella, a rewarding 4-hour trek along active railway tracks, through rubber and eucalyptus forests, to a dizzying 1,000m sheer cliff precipice.

Hiking & Trekking
Badulla District
Best Season: Early morning start (06:00 - 07:00) is strongly recommended to summit before midday heat and afternoon cloud cover roll in.
Duration: 4 - 5 Hours (Round Trip)
Popular Highlight

About Ella Rock Summit Trek & Ella Gap

Looming with dramatic, imposing majesty on the southern side of the Ella Gap, Ella Rock is the undisputed king of trekking trails in the Uva highlands. Reaching the summit requires an adventurous, scenic 4-hour round-trip trek (approx. 8 to 10 kilometers) starting along the active railway tracks from Ella railway station. Hikers cross a picturesque metal railway bridge over a roaring mountain stream, navigate winding trails through village gardens, tea plantations, and tall eucalyptus forests, before conquering a steep, root-strewn dirt scramble to the summit plateau. Perched at an elevation of 1,350 meters above sea level, the cliff-top precipice of Ella Rock plunges over 1,000 meters straight down into the Ella Gap, offering staggering, dizzying panoramas across the southern plains, Little Adam's Peak, and distant tea highlands.

Historical & Cultural Background

A historic natural cliff lookout commanding the ancient southern trade route between the highlands and Ruhuna.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

Massive granite cliff precipice rising 1,350m elevation with eucalyptus forests, scrub grasslands, and montane tea fields.

What Makes It Special

Dizzying 1,000-meter sheer cliff drop-off, railway track trek segment, and 360-degree viewpoint plateau.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Stand on the thrilling, sheer cliff edge of Ella Rock looking 1,000 meters down into the Ella Gap
  • Experience a classic Sri Lankan adventure hiking along railway tracks, across bridges, and through pine forests
  • Enjoy breathtaking, unobstructed views of Little Adam's Peak and the southern ocean horizon
  • Conquer Ella's most famous and physically rewarding hiking summit

Things to Do & Available Experiences

Challenging mountain trekking
Cliff edge landscape photography
Railway walking segment
Picnicking on the summit plateau

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Sturdy hiking shoes with good tread, at least 1.5 liters of drinking water, snacks, sun hat, camera, phone with offline GPS map.
Recommended Clothing: Breathable trekking pants or shorts and athletic t-shirt; light windbreaker.
Photography Advice: Shoot from the rocky outcrop slightly to the right of the main summit to capture a hiker sitting on the cliff edge with the infinite valley below.
Local Customs: Politely decline unauthorized trail 'helpers' asking for money, or agree on a clear fee upfront with a genuine licensed local guide.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Strict Leave No Trace policy; carry all plastic and waste back down to Ella town.

Visitor Information

District Badulla District
Province Uva Province
Opening Hours Open daylight hours (06:00 - 18:00).
Entrance Fee Nominal viewpoint maintenance ticket: approx. LKR 1,000 at the summit checkpoint.
Best Season Early morning start (06:00 - 07:00) is strongly recommended to summit before midday heat and afternoon cloud cover roll in.
Visiting Duration 4 - 5 Hours (Round Trip)
Accessibility Strenuous hike with steep, slippery root-strewn climbs and uneven rock paths; requires good physical fitness; not wheelchair accessible.
Safety Advice Trail markings can be confusing with misleading paths; use GPS mapping (like Maps.me/Google Maps) or hire a local guide; keep well back from sheer unfenced cliff edges.
