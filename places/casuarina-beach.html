Casuarina Beach (Karainagar Island)

The most famous beach in the north, celebrated for fine white sands, shallow turquoise waters where you can walk hundreds of meters out, and whispering casuarina trees.

Beaches & Coastlines
Jaffna District
Best Season: January to September (calm seas, crystal clear shallows, sunny warm weather).
Duration: 2 - 4 Hours
Popular Highlight

About Casuarina Beach (Karainagar Island)

Situated on the northern shore of Karainagar Island, connected to the Jaffna peninsula by a scenic 5-kilometer causeway across the lagoon, Casuarina Beach is widely regarded as the premier beach destination in the Northern Province. Named after the distinctive rows of whispering casuarina (ironwood) trees that line the powdery white sand, the beach is renowned for its extraordinarily shallow, tranquil, crystal-clear turquoise waters. Visitors can safely walk several hundred meters out into the Palk Bay with the warm water barely reaching waist-deep, making it an idyllic, wave-free natural playground for families and swimmers of all abilities.

Historical & Cultural Background

A historic recreational coastal spot for northern communities, modernized with visitor amenities following the restoration of peace in 2009.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

Broad white sand beach bordered by shallow coral sandflats and planted casuarina coastal trees.

What Makes It Special

Extremely shallow, crystal-clear water extending hundreds of meters offshore, casuarina tree shade, and local catamaran boat rides.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Wade into serene, transparent turquoise waters stretching hundreds of meters with no waves or drop-offs
  • Relax beneath the cool shade of whistling casuarina trees with refreshing sea breezes
  • Enjoy the most popular and family-safe swimming beach in northern Sri Lanka
  • Cross the scenic Karainagar causeway with panoramic views of traditional crab and prawn fishermen

Things to Do & Available Experiences

Shallow water swimming and wading
Beach relaxation
Catamaran coastal boat rides
Photography of white sands

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Swimwear, beach towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, drinking water.
Recommended Clothing: Modest beachwear; cover up with a t-shirt or sarong when leaving the sand.
Photography Advice: Capture the contrast of emerald green casuarina branches framing the white sand and glowing turquoise water.
Local Customs: Enjoy fresh palmyra fruit and cool coconut water sold by vendors near the parking area.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Strict zero-litter policy; take all plastics back to municipal bins.
