Kitulgala White Water Rafting & Kelani Rainforest

Sri Lanka's adrenaline adventure capital on the rushing Kelani River, famed for Grade II-IV white-water rafting, canyoning, and filming location of 'The Bridge on the River Kwai'.

About Kitulgala White Water Rafting & Kelani Rainforest

Nestled in the lush, rainforest-clad foothills of the western highlands in Kegalle District, Kitulgala is the undisputed adventure tourism capital of Sri Lanka. Through this verdant canyon rushes the mighty Kelani River, creating an exhilarating 5-kilometer stretch of Grade II and Grade III (and occasional Grade IV during high water) white-water rapids bearing colorful names like 'Head Chopper', 'Virgin's Breast', 'Butter Crunch', and 'Killer Fall'. Expert international-standard guides navigate rafters through roaring chutes of white water, followed by refreshing flat-water river swimming. Beyond rafting, Kitulgala offers thrilling canyoning, waterfall abseiling, rainforest trekking in the Makandawa Forest Reserve (home to 54 rare bird species), and visits to the historical filming site where director David Lean shot his iconic 1957 Oscar-winning classic 'The Bridge on the River Kwai'.

Historical & Cultural Background

Filming location of the 1957 Hollywood masterpiece 'The Bridge on the River Kwai'; ancient prehistoric settlement site of Belilena.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

Deep rushing river canyon surrounded by dense wet-zone tropical rainforest and bamboo thickets.

What Makes It Special

Grade II-III white-water rapids, natural rock waterfall abseiling, canyoning cliff jumps, and Makandawa Forest bird sanctuary.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Experience thrilling Grade II-III white-water rafting down the roaring Kelani River canyon
  • Jump off natural rock waterfalls and slide down smooth natural rock water slides
  • Stand at the historical filming location of Oscar-winning film 'The Bridge on the River Kwai'
  • Trek through the untouched virgin canopy of Makandawa Rainforest to spot rare endemic birds

Things to Do & Available Experiences

White-water rafting
Canyoning and waterfall abseiling
Rainforest trekking in Makandawa
River swimming and cliff jumping

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Swimwear, quick-dry shorts/t-shirt, water shoes or old sneakers with grip (flip-flops not permitted on rafts), change of dry clothes, towel, GoPro/waterproof camera.
Recommended Clothing: Quick-drying athletic clothes or swimwear.
Photography Advice: Mount a waterproof action camera on your helmet to record first-person footage plunging through the roaring rapids.
Local Customs: Enjoy a traditional rice and curry lunch wrapped in banana leaves at riverside adventure camps.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Keep the pristine Kelani River canyon free of plastic waste.

Visitor Information

District Kegalle District
Province Sabaragamuwa Province
Opening Hours Rafting operations run 08:00 - 17:00 Daily.
Entrance Fee Rafting excursions cost approx. LKR 3,500 - 5,000 per person (includes safety gear, helmets, life jackets, and guide); canyoning packages extra.
Best Season Year-round; water levels are thrilling from May to December after monsoon rains; January to April offers gentle family rafting.
Visiting Duration Half Day to Full Day Adventure
Accessibility Requires boarding inflatable rafts from riverbanks and walking along rocky paths; reasonable agility needed.
Safety Advice Always choose accredited operators with certified river guides; wearing certified life jackets and helmets is mandatory at all times on the river.
