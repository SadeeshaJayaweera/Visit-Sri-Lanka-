Bahirawakanda Vihara Buddha Statue

A commanding 27-meter colossal white seated Buddha statue atop Bahirawa Kanda hill, offering panoramic views over the entire city of Kandy.

Temples & Religious Sites
Kandy District
Best Season: Late afternoon (16:30 - 18:30) to experience both daylight views and twilight city lights.
Duration: 1 Hour
Popular Highlight

About Bahirawakanda Vihara Buddha Statue

Gazing serenely over Kandy from the summit of Bahirawa Kanda (Gnome Mountain), the giant Sri Maha Bodhi Vihara Buddha statue is one of the hill capital's most visible landmarks. Standing an impressive 26.8 meters (88 feet) tall in the Dhyana Mudra (meditation posture), this brilliant white statue was completed in 1993. Visitors climb a winding road up the hill, followed by stairs behind the Buddha's back to reach a gallery near the statue's shoulders. From this elevated platform, the vista opens onto an unforgettable panorama of Kandy Lake, the Temple of the Tooth, and the surrounding amphitheater of green mountain ridges.

Historical & Cultural Background

Built atop Bahirawakanda hill, a site steeped in ancient folklore; the modern colossal statue was consecrated in 1993.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

Hilltop promontory rising above the Kandy city basin.

What Makes It Special

27-meter tall white seated Buddha statue and panoramic city observation gallery.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Climb to the observation gallery behind the Buddha's shoulders for the best aerial view of Kandy
  • Marvel at the monumental scale of the 88-foot white seated Buddha colossus
  • Experience twilight when the statue is bathed in warm floodlights and temple bells ring
  • Escape the city bustle for a contemplative spiritual atmosphere

Things to Do & Available Experiences

Statue observation
City skyline photography
Quiet contemplation
Temple exploration

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Modest clothing, camera, slip-off shoes.
Recommended Clothing: Shoulders and knees must be respectfully covered.
Photography Advice: Shoot looking down toward Kandy Lake with the temple precinct in the foreground at dusk.
Local Customs: Do not pose with your back turned to the Buddha for selfies.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Keep the temple grounds clean and peaceful.
