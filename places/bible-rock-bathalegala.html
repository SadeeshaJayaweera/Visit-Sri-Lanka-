Bible Rock (Bathalegala Mountain Citadel)

A dramatic 798-meter flat-topped table mountain resembling an open book, offering an exhilarating ridge hike, ancient rock caves, and 360-degree vistas.

Mountains & Highlands
Kegalle District
Best Season: December to April (clear sunny weather, dry trail conditions; start early morning by 07:00).
Duration: 3 - 4 Hours
Hidden Gem

About Bible Rock (Bathalegala Mountain Citadel)

Rising solitary and majestic to an elevation of 798 meters (2,618 feet) out of the Kegalle valleys near Aranayaka, Bathalegala (famously dubbed 'Bible Rock' by British colonial surveyors because its flat-topped silhouette resembles an open book or Bible) is one of the most distinctive geological formations in Sri Lanka. Similar in shape to the famous Table Mountain of Cape Town or Sigiriya Rock, the mountain is a steep-sided granite fortress with a remarkably flat summit plateau. A scenic 2 to 3-hour hiking trail winds through village rubber gardens, climbs steep stone staircases along forested cliff ledges, and scales the final rock face to reach the summit. At the top sits a peaceful Buddhist meditation hermitage and an observation point offering awe-inspiring, unobstructed 360-degree panoramas of Adam's Peak, Ambuluwawa tower, the Knuckles range, and the winding green valleys of Kegalle.

Historical & Cultural Background

Used as a natural defensive fortress and signaling lookout by Kandyan and Gampola kings; named Bible Rock by 19th-century British surveyors.

Natural & Geographical Characteristics

Prominent flat-topped table mountain inselberg with sheer vertical rock cliffs and a forested summit plateau.

What Makes It Special

Distinctive flat-topped 'Bible' silhouette, rock meditation hermitage, and 360-degree highland panoramas.

Why You Shouldn't Miss This Place

  • Hike up one of Sri Lanka's most iconic and unique table mountains
  • Enjoy breathtaking 360-degree panoramas extending across four provinces and to Adam's Peak
  • Experience a peaceful summit plateau with a quiet Buddhist rock hermitage
  • Escape tourist crowds on an authentic, rewarding central hiking adventure

Things to Do & Available Experiences

Mountain summit hiking
Panoramic 360-degree photography
Meditation on the plateau
Birdwatching

Insider Travel Tips

What to Bring: Sturdy hiking shoes, drinking water (at least 1.5 liters), sun hat, camera.
Recommended Clothing: Comfortable hiking clothing; cover shoulders and knees at the summit temple.
Photography Advice: Photograph the famous flat-topped table silhouette of Bible Rock from the Kadugannawa pass, and the 360-degree view from the summit looking toward Adam's Peak.
Local Customs: Quiet reverence around the resident monks' hermitage.
Eco & Conservation Guidelines: Strict Leave No Trace policy; carry all trash back down.
